Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about referring

How do I get my referral link? +
After you complete a booking, your unique referral link appears on the confirmation page (success page). You can copy it and share it anywhere.
How do I use my coupon code? +
When a friend books using your link, we email your coupon code. On your next booking, simply enter the code in the "Referral code" field on the booking form and your discount will be applied.
Is there a limit to how many friends I can refer? +
No limit! Refer as many friends as you like. You'll earn a coupon for every friend who books. Check your credits below to see your balance.
Can I use multiple coupon codes? +
One coupon per booking. But if you have multiple credits, they accumulate — use them one at a time across your future sessions.
